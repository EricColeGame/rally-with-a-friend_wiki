## Troubleshooting Common Progression Roadblocks

Earning every trophy can be frustrating if technical issues or miscommunications disrupt your momentum. Based on community reports, several players encounter tracking bugs or synchronization hiccups during extended cross-platform play sessions.

### Handling Desync and Progression Tracking

* **Host-Client Discrepancies:** If an achievement fails to trigger for the client player, verify that lobby progression syncing is enabled before starting the stage.
* **Mid-Stage Disconnects:** If a teammate disconnects during a multi-stage endurance run, restart the specific stage from the paddock menu rather than abandoning the championship to protect stage completion counters.
* **Role Inversion Checks:** Certain role-specific milestones require the host to play as co-driver. Swap host privileges if an unlock fails to register after completing all criteria.

Following these checks ensures that secondary drivers also pop **RALLY WITH A FRIEND achievements** without repeating full campaign arcs unnecessarily.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do both players unlock RALLY WITH A FRIEND achievements simultaneously in online lobbies?
Yes, the vast majority of trophies unlock concurrently for both the driver and the navigator, provided both players are present from the stage countdown to the final marshal checkpoint. However, role-specific awards (such as delivering a set number of pace notes) may require alternating roles so each player meets individual criteria.

### Can RALLY WITH A FRIEND achievements be completed in split-screen mode?
Most progression milestones are fully compatible with local split-screen co-op. Make sure both participants are logged into active individual player profiles rather than a temporary guest account so secondary progression saves properly.

### What is the most difficult achievement in the game?
Community members generally agree that endurance challenges requiring night driving in severe storms without vehicle assists stand out as the toughest hurdles. These runs demand flawless mechanical conservation and absolute trust between driver and co-driver.

### Are driving assists permitted when hunting time trial achievements?
Assists like ABS and stability control can typically remain active, but they often restrict your top-end speed on loose gravel or deep snow. Disabling traction control allows experienced duos to rotate the car quicker, making it significantly easier to check off endgame **RALLY WITH A FRIEND achievements** efficiently.
